This Cookie Policy explains how the scoped management plane uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website and use our Services.

1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They are used to remember preferences, maintain sessions, and provide functionality.

2. Cookies we use

Essential cookies

These cookies are required for the Services to function. They cannot be disabled.

  • __session — Maintains your authenticated session with the management plane. Set by our authentication provider (Clerk). Expires when the session ends.
  • csrftoken — Protects against cross-site request forgery attacks. Set by Django. Expires after one year.

Authentication cookies

These cookies are set by our authentication provider (Clerk) to manage sign-in state.

  • __clerk_db_jwt — Clerk session management token.
  • __client_uat — Clerk client update timestamp.

3. Third-party services

We use the following third-party services that may set cookies:

  • Clerk — Authentication and user management. See Clerk's Privacy Policy.

4. Managing cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies will prevent you from signing in to the management plane. The scoped SDK does not use cookies and is unaffected by browser cookie settings.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as we add new features or third-party integrations. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

6. Contact

For questions about this Cookie Policy, contact us at privacy@kwip.info.
