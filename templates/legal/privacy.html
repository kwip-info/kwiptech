Privacy Policy

Updated September 7, 2026.

PyScoped 2.0 sends no records, audit events, or telemetry to KWIP. Its data remains in your application’s database. Hosted PyScoped ingestion and account creation are retired. Any historical platform records remain access restricted during deprecation; retirement does not imply their deletion.

If you submit a Digest pilot inquiry, we store the contact information and project details you provide to respond and manage that inquiry. Do not submit confidential documents or credentials. Authorized KWIP staff can access these inquiries.

Heroku hosts this website and its database; Cloudflare provides traffic delivery and security. These providers may process request and security logs. The site also loads presentation assets from third-party CDNs and Google Fonts, which receive browser network requests. We no longer load Clerk or Stripe for the retired platform.

We use necessary session and CSRF cookies for forms and staff administration. Contact hello@kwip.info to ask about your information or request its deletion, subject to applicable recordkeeping obligations.
