This Privacy Policy describes how KWIP LLC ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the scoped management plane and related services ("Services").

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and authentication credentials through our identity provider (Clerk). We store your account identifier and associate it with your usage data.

Synced metadata

The sync agent transmits structural metadata from your scoped SDK instance to the management plane. This includes: action types, timestamps, actor identifiers, target object types, target identifiers, sequence numbers, and chain hashes. This metadata describes the structure of operations, not the content of your data.

Object data payloads (the contents of objects you create, update, or manage with the SDK) are never transmitted to the management plane.

Usage data

We collect usage metrics including active object counts, active principal counts, and sync frequency for billing and analytics purposes.

2. How we use information

  • To provide and maintain the management plane dashboard and API
  • To calculate and process billing based on usage
  • To generate compliance reports and audit trail search results
  • To monitor service health and performance
  • To communicate with you about your account and the Services

3. Data residency

Your application data processed by the SDK remains in your infrastructure at all times. The management plane stores only structural metadata received through the sync agent. This metadata is stored in our database infrastructure.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell your information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who assist in operating the management plane (hosting, payment processing)
  • Law enforcement when required by law or to protect our rights

5. Data retention

Synced metadata is retained according to your plan's audit retention settings. Account information is retained while your account is active and for 30 days after termination. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

6. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit (TLS), HMAC signature verification on all synced data, and hash-chain integrity verification. See our Security page for details.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Export your synced metadata
  • Withdraw consent for optional data processing

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via email or through the dashboard.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at contact@kwip.info.
