Product security architecture

Data residency

PyScoped 2.0 runs in your Django application and writes audit history to your database in the same transaction. KWIP receives no records or telemetry from the library. Trusted application code supplies actor and scope after authorization.

PyScoped protects supported registered-model ORM paths. Raw SQL, private Django APIs, unregistered models, and database administrators are outside that boundary. Hash chains detect alteration; they do not prevent a database administrator rewriting a whole chain. Read the guarantees and limitations.

Digest runtime boundary

Digest processes documents in the customer’s environment. This website receives pilot contact details, not customer documents. Please do not put document contents or credentials into the pilot form.
