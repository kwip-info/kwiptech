Security and trust

scoped is built on the principle that your data belongs in your infrastructure. We designed every layer to enforce this guarantee.

Architecture

The scoped SDK runs entirely in your application process, against your database. All CRUD operations, versioning, audit trail recording, access control, and rule evaluation execute locally. The management plane is a read-only view — it never writes to your database and cannot modify your data.

Data residency

Your application data never leaves your infrastructure. The sync agent pushes only structural metadata to the management plane: who performed an action, when, on what type of object, and the chain hash for integrity verification. Object data payloads (the before_state and after_state fields) are deliberately excluded from sync. Your data stays in your database, under your control.

Sync security

TLS encryption

All communication between the sync agent and the management plane is encrypted in transit via TLS.

HMAC-SHA256 signatures

Every sync batch is signed with HMAC-SHA256 using a key derived from your API key. The management plane verifies the signature before accepting any data.

Hash-chained audit trail

Each audit entry includes a hash of the previous entry, creating a tamper-evident chain. The management plane verifies chain integrity on every sync.

Content hashing

Each batch includes a SHA256 hash of its contents, ensuring nothing was modified in transit independent of the transport layer.

Secrets at rest

API keys are encrypted at rest using scoped's built-in secrets vault (AES-128-CBC with HMAC-SHA256 via Fernet). Plaintext keys are shown exactly once at creation time through a short-lived, audited access reference. Every access attempt is logged — you can see exactly who viewed a key and when. After the reveal, the plaintext is inaccessible. We use the same secrets vault we sell to protect our own credentials.

Compliance invariants

The scoped framework enforces ten absolute guarantees at every layer. These invariants are checked by the compliance engine and cannot be bypassed.

Nothing exists without registration

Nothing happens without identity

Nothing is shared by default

Nothing happens without a trace

Nothing is truly deleted

Deny always wins

Revocation is immediate

Everything is versioned

Everything is rollbackable

Secrets never leak
