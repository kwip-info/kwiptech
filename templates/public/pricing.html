What is never billed

Audit entry count, operation count, and API calls are never billed. We do not punish usage of core features. Your local SDK always continues to function regardless of billing status.

Frequently asked questions

How is usage measured?
We measure peak active objects and principals per billing period. "Active" means non-tombstoned resources in your scoped database. We bill on the highest count seen during the period, not the average.
What happens when I exceed my plan?
On the Free plan, sync batches are rejected when you exceed limits. On Pro, you're never blocked — overage is tracked and billed at the per-unit rate.
Can I downgrade?
Yes. Downgrade at any time from the billing page. Your current billing period completes at the Pro rate, then Free limits apply.
