How scoped works

01

SDK in your infrastructure

The scoped SDK runs in your application process. All CRUD, versioning, audit, and access control execute locally against your database.

02

Sync agent pushes metadata

A lightweight daemon syncs structural metadata to the management plane. Who did what, when, to what type of thing. Never the data itself.

03

Dashboard gives you visibility

The management plane provides audit trail search, compliance reports, usage analytics, and team management. A read-only view into your operations.
