CONTENT & CREDITS

A little context
for the unexpected.

Real images, real sources.

Collection images come from Wikimedia Commons and the Art Institute of Chicago. Each image slide links to its original record, creator, and license. Images are scaled to fit, without cropping or recoloring. Different images have different reuse terms: follow the license linked on each slide.

Our importer accepts Commons records with explicit public-domain, CC0, or supported Creative Commons Attribution licenses, and Art Institute works marked public domain. Credits reflect the provider’s metadata. Report a rights or attribution concern to contact@kwip.info with the slide’s source link.

Some things are deliberately made up.

KWIP’s original prompts are invitations to improvise. Chart numbers are invented for the performance, not research findings. A small collection of original images was generated with OpenAI; those slides explicitly say “AI-generated illustration.” They do not depict real events or objects.

Take the credits with you.

Press S during a presentation, or choose Sources, to inspect attribution. Finish opens downloads for readable credits and a full JSON session record. If you publish a recording, retain the required credits and license links. Downloads include only slides actually shown, in their presented order.

Endless combinations, a finite collection.

The catalog contains {{ count }} items, recombined with layouts, colors, and styles. Content can eventually repeat. The player avoids recent repeats and spreads selections across subjects; it does not plan connections between slides. Each session supports up to 5,000 slides before asking you to start a new ride.

Your session stays in your browser.

No account, payment, or saved cloud session is required. We don’t add tracking scripts or cookies. Heroku handles ordinary hosting requests and logs. Loading external images sends ordinary request information, including your IP address, to their image providers. Refreshing or starting again clears the in-memory presentation, so download your credits first.
