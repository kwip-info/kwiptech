{% if kind == 'privacy' %}

KWIP stores your account identifier, workspace settings, hashed API credentials, authorization approvals, query delivery receipts, usage totals, and billing references to operate this marketplace. Clerk provides account authentication. Stripe processes subscriptions and payments; KWIP does not store card details.

Heroku hosts the application and database. Cloudflare provides traffic delivery and security. Query response caches and export files can contain requested records. They expire after 24 hours and the background worker removes their payloads; delivery identifiers and accounting totals remain for billing and replay protection. Backups can retain prior copies until their normal expiry. Billing and security records can be retained longer for accounting, abuse prevention, and disputes.

Dataset records are governed by the source and license information shown on each dataset. Contact us about incorrect records, access restrictions, or removal requests. Do not submit credentials or sensitive personal data through publisher endpoints unless explicitly approved for the dataset and its intended distribution.

{% else %}

KWIP Data provides access to published datasets under their stated source and license conditions. Public catalog descriptions do not guarantee completeness, historical coverage, accuracy, or fitness for a particular decision. Review provenance and coverage before relying on records.

Catalog metadata is free to browse. Record retrieval consumes the displayed credits. The pricing page describes included allowances and Pro features. Paid overage requires explicit opt-in and a spending cap; it is off by default. Stripe presents subscription and payment terms before purchase. No paid purchase is available while the catalog is empty.

Keep credentials private and stay within the scopes and limits granted to your account. Do not bypass access controls or redistribute records beyond the dataset’s license. Publisher access is restricted to authorized KWIP operators. Source permissions must be established before data is published.

Rejected queries and empty results use no credits. A lost response may follow a committed delivery; retry using the original idempotency key. Identical retries with the original idempotency key reuse the original receipt within its retention window. Export preparation consumes credits for the records prepared; downloading the prepared export does not charge again. Contact KWIP with receipt identifiers for billing questions.
